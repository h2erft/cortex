How to connect (read first)

Safety: measure the signal voltage with a multimeter before wiring — LG service UART is likely 5V or 3.3V TTL. Use an adapter that matches (or a level shifter). Connect GND first, then the adapter's RX to the bus TX. For a pure sniff, leave the adapter's TX disconnected and don't connect VCC.
1 · Plug in your USB-to-TTL adapter (a CP2102 one matches what LGMV uses).
2 · Pick the baud (LG's ODU UART baud isn't published — start at 38400, then try 115200, 57600, 19200, 9600). The Structure helper tells you when a baud looks right.
3 · Click Connect and choose the adapter's COM port.
4 · To decode fields later: run LG's LGMV at the same time, hit Start recording here, and drop a Marker each time you change something (turn an IDU on, change setpoint). Afterward, line the markers up against LGMV's exported CSV.
